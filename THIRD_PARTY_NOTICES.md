# Attribution and third-party notices

The paper bibliography cites the models, inventories, social-cognition tasks, and related methods used in the study. BFI-10 follows Rammstedt and John (2007); the LLM adaptation cites PsychoBench. Conformity prompts use an Asch-inspired paired-pressure design; wording probes cite CogMir as methodological inspiration.

The original project README attributes design influence or adaptation to HyperGraphRAG (graph memory and retrieval), FutureMap (reasoning/experience components), KnowledgeClaw (memory extraction), MiroFish (agent scaffolding), and OASIS (probe and trace patterns). That attribution is preserved here. The available workspace does not establish a complete upstream file-by-file license provenance record.

Models are accessed through their providers' APIs under each provider's terms: OpenAI (GPT-4.1-mini), Google (Gemini-2.5-Flash), DeepSeek (DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash), and OpenRouter (Llama-4-Scout, Seed-2.0-mini, GLM-4.7-Flash); the locally served Qwen models keep their released licenses.

Runtime dependencies are installed separately and retain their own licenses. This package distributes no LLM model weights. Model access and redistribution terms belong to their respective providers and model authors.
